Operating Conditions

What this is

A conceptual simulator. It makes policy, topology, membership, and transcript residue legible.

What this is not

No cryptography, no network stack, no production claims, and no attempt to be a generic graph playground.

What to watch for

Stale participants do not count. Revoked participants do not count. Fake diversity fails. The transcript leaves a durable scar in the log.

Gossip

Gossip tells the system who seems to exist and what claims are moving. It is epistemic, not sufficient for authority.

Consensus

Consensus decides which transitions count as shared history. It does not by itself force hidden capability through plural contact.

Threshold Ceremony

Threshold ceremony constrains when latent authority may become observable consequence, and under what transcript of collective presence.

Membership / Topology State

Nodes carry role, domain, epoch, and contact surface. Select participants here, then ask whether the chosen quorum deserves causal force.

Quorum Policy / Ceremony

Choose a scenario, inspect the active policy, advance the epoch, then attempt reveal. The middle panel exists to make the policy surface explicit.

Observable Consequence / Transcript

The payload matters, but the transcript matters too. A valid ceremony leaves evidence that authority crossed into the world through plural contact.

A capability exists here only as a distributed social fact. The simulator is deliberately explicit about residue, because silent scars become governance debt later.
