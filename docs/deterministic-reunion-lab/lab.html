Merge / Healing / Residue

Deterministic Reunion Lab

A local-only proof-object for a harder healing claim: partition healing is not connectivity restored, therefore truth restored. Healing is a negotiated merge of partially divergent realities, with residue preserved rather than flattened away.

Restored contact is only the start. The real work is deciding what may converge, what must remain visible residue, and what kind of scar the repair digest should leave behind for operators later.

Operating Conditions

What this is

A conceptual reunion lab. It visualizes divergent local views, explicit merge rules, residue, and operator-visible repair artifacts.

What this is not

No network runtime, no hidden backend, no fake cryptography, and no promise of perfect reconciliation.

Difference from Quorum Lab

Quorum Lab asks when hidden capability may enter the world. Deterministic Reunion Lab asks how a system should behave after it has already learned its observers can diverge.

Residue

Residue is unresolved disagreement retained as visible structure. It is not failure. It is the protocol refusing to counterfeit certainty.

RepairDigest

A compact operator-facing explanation of what inputs were merged, which rules fired, what stayed conflicting, and whether the outcome is stable or still provisional.

OperatorOverride

A constrained human intervention for cases the merge should not finish silently. It leaves an explicit scar in the digest rather than pretending the system healed itself.
